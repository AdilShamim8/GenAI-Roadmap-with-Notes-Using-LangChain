# From Frontend Engineer

> **Level:** BEG → ADV · **Time to job-ready:** 16 weeks (10h/week)

## Your unfair advantages

- You understand UX, latency budgets, streaming UIs.
- You can build the agent-facing UI that makes or breaks adoption.
- You're comfortable with TypeScript and the Vercel AI SDK / Mastra ecosystem.

## Your gaps

- Backend depth (auth, queues, async jobs, durable execution).
- Probability, evaluation metrics, statistics for evals.
- Deployment beyond Vercel.

## Projects to prioritize

1. **Project 6: Real-time voice assistant** — your UX chops win here.
2. **Project 14: Multimodal customer-support agent** — full-stack agent with a real UI.
3. **Project 1: Structured-output extractor** — quick win, builds confidence.
