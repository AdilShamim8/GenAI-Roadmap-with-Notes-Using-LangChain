# From Data Engineer

> **Level:** BEG → ADV · **Time to job-ready:** 14 weeks (10h/week)

## Your unfair advantages

- You live in pipelines, batch/stream, schemas, lineage.
- RAG is your world with a new index type.
- Evals feel like data quality checks.

## Your gaps

- LLM-specific brittleness (non-determinism, prompt sensitivity).
- Model selection beyond "use the biggest."
- Cost economics of tokens vs compute.
