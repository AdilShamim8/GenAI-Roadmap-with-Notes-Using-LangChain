## Equity reality

- **Public company:** Equity is liquid; assume 4-year vest, 1-year cliff.
- **Late-stage startup (Series C+):** Some liquidity via tender offers; otherwise illiquid.
- **Early-stage:** Treat as a lottery ticket. Negotiate cash up.
- **AI-specific:** Watch valuation hype; 2023-era AI startups marked at 100× ARR may not survive repricing.

## Negotiation

- **Always negotiate.** First offer is rarely best.
- **Multiple offers are leverage.** Even one competing offer moves comp 10–30%.
- **Equity is negotiable too.** Especially at startups.
- **Sign-on bonus is a one-time lever.** Use it to bridge a base gap.
- **Don't accept "we don't negotiate."** Almost always false.

## Contracting / consulting

- **Independent AI eng day rate (US):** $800–$2,500/day.
- **Long-term engagement (3+ months):** Lower day rate, more stability.
- **Equity-only:** Almost never worth it.
