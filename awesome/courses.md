# Courses

> Verified 2026-07-30.

## Free

- [DeepLearning.AI Short Courses](https://www.deeplearning.ai/short-courses/) — Free, project-based, frequently updated. Best starting point.
- [LangChain Academy](https://academy.langchain.com/) — Free official LangChain/LangGraph courses.
- [Andrej Karpathy's YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@AndrejKarpathy) — "Let's build GPT" and "Deep Dive into LLMs like ChatGPT" are required viewing.
- [HuggingFace LLM Course](https://huggingface.co/learn/llm-course) — Free, well-structured.
- [Anthropic prompt engineering tutorial](https://github.com/anthropics/courses) — Official, free.
- [OpenAI Cookbook](https://cookbook.openai.com/) — Not a course but a curated recipe set; required reference.

## Paid

- [AI Engineering Buildcamp — Alexey Grigorev](https://maven.com/alexey-grigorev/from-rag-to-agents) — 9-week intensive; the closest to "production GenAI engineering" you'll find.
- [Maven courses by Chip Huyen / others](https://maven.com/) — Variable quality; pick carefully.
- [Fast.ai](https://www.fast.ai/) — Foundational deep learning. Free.
